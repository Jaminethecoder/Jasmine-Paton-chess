Beginner lessons

Learn how the pieces move, spot checkmate, and discover the basics through friendly, hands-on practice.

  • Rules and piece movement
  • Checks, checkmates, and tactics
  • Confidence at the board

Developing players

Build stronger habits and learn how to turn good ideas into a plan during a game.

  • Opening principles and strategy
  • Reviewing games and learning why

Tournament preparation

Get ready to play with a clear head, thoughtful routines, and a plan for learning from every result.

  • Practice positions and game review
  • Clock and tournament habits
  • Preparation matched to your goals
